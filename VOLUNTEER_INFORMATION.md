# Volunteer Information — Planning Document

This document is a planning template. It does not mean that HealthNGO is currently accepting volunteers.

## Purpose
If the organization launches a volunteer programme, this page should explain its goals, available roles, eligibility, expectations, and application process.

## Possible role categories
Select only roles the organization can supervise and support:
- Social media and content support
- Event assistance
- Translation and accessibility support
- Website and technical contributions
- Community outreach, where appropriately trained and authorized

## Suggested process
1. Publish an official volunteer call with clear dates and role descriptions.
2. Collect only necessary information through a secure channel.
3. Explain how applicant information will be used and retained.
4. Review applications consistently and respectfully.
5. Provide role-specific orientation and supervision.
6. Maintain a safe way to raise concerns.

## Safeguarding
- Do not ask volunteers to provide medical, legal, or other specialist services unless properly qualified and authorized.
- Establish supervision and safeguarding procedures before community-facing activities.
- Protect the privacy and dignity of people who may receive support.
- Do not publish volunteer or beneficiary personal details without appropriate consent.

## Details to confirm before launch
- Volunteer coordinator and official contact
- Age and eligibility requirements
- Roles, locations, schedules, and training
- Expense and reimbursement policy, if any
- Safeguarding and complaints process
- Data collection, privacy notice, and retention period
