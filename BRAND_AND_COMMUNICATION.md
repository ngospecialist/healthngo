## Voice and tone
- Respectful and community-focused
- Clear, warm, and factual
- Hopeful without exaggerating outcomes
- Inclusive and non-stigmatizing
- Transparent about what is planned versus completed

## Writing principles
1. Prefer plain language.
2. Explain unfamiliar terms.
3. Avoid sensational headlines.
4. Do not promise outcomes the organization cannot guarantee.
5. Attribute statistics and factual claims to credible sources.
6. Use the organization's approved name and contact details consistently.

## Visual principles
- Use approved, high-quality images.
- Ensure text is readable on mobile screens.
- Add meaningful alternative text.
- Obtain permission before using identifiable people's images or stories.
- Do not use stock imagery in a way that implies it depicts actual beneficiaries or activities.
