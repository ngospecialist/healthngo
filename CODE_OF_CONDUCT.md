# Community Code of Conduct

HealthNGO welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful, kind, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on ideas and work, not individuals.
- Welcome questions and differing viewpoints.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks.
- Respect the privacy and dignity of communities and individuals represented in project content.

## Unacceptable behavior

Public or private harassment, hateful language, intimidation, doxxing, sharing private information, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior in this project, contact the project maintainers through the repository's private contact route. Do not post sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not follow these expectations.
