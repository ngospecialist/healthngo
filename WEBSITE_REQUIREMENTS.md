# Website Requirements — Initial Specification

## Project
HealthNGO public information website.

## Primary objective
Provide a reliable, accessible online home for verified organizational information, awareness resources, and public updates.

## Initial release: suggested scope
- Responsive homepage
- About page
- Our Work / Initiatives page
- Awareness articles or resources
- News and updates
- Contact information
- Links to verified social media profiles

## Possible later features
These are ideas only and should be prioritized after requirements, privacy, staffing, and budget are confirmed:
- Content management system
- Volunteer enquiry form
- Newsletter subscription
- Event calendar
- Donation information or payment integration, subject to legal and operational checks
- Multiple language support

## Non-functional requirements
- Mobile-first responsive layout
- Accessible navigation and readable content
- HTTPS
- Search-engine-friendly page titles and descriptions
- Optimized images and reasonable page performance
- Backups and update process
- Secure admin access if a CMS is used
- Privacy notice and consent controls appropriate to actual data collection

## Acceptance criteria
- Visitors can understand the NGO's verified purpose.
- Navigation works on mobile and desktop.
- Contact details and social links are correct.
- No placeholder text remains on production pages.
- No unverified claims or private personal information are published.
- Forms, if any, are tested and have clear privacy information.

## Open decisions
- Approved organization description and focus areas
- Languages
- Domain and hosting
- Preferred technology stack
- Content owner and update frequency
- Whether the first release needs any forms
