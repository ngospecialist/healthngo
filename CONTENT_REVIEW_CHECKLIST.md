# Content Review Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing a website page, social media post, campaign announcement, or public report.

## Accuracy
- [ ] Names, dates, locations, numbers, and links are checked.
- [ ] Factual claims have reliable sources.
- [ ] Health or medical information has been reviewed by an appropriate qualified source.
- [ ] No unverified partnership, registration, award, or endorsement is claimed.

## Consent and privacy
- [ ] Permission has been obtained for identifiable photos, quotes, and personal stories.
- [ ] No private phone numbers, addresses, identity documents, or medical details are exposed.
- [ ] Children's images or stories have additional appropriate safeguards and consent.
- [ ] Location metadata and background details do not expose anyone to risk.

## Accessibility
- [ ] Language is clear and respectful.
- [ ] Images have useful alternative text where possible.
- [ ] Videos have captions.
- [ ] Text is readable and has sufficient contrast.
- [ ] Essential information is not available only inside an image.

## Transparency
- [ ] The post clearly distinguishes plans from completed work.
- [ ] Donation or fundraising details, if any, have been verified and approved.
- [ ] Corrections contact or source details are included where appropriate.
